Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026
The short version: I collect only what you give me through the audit form or by emailing me. I use it to reply to you and prepare your recommendations. I never sell it, and I never share it with anyone who isn't helping me run Wise AI. This site has no tracking cookies and no advertising pixels.

Who I am

Wise AI is a business operated by Tyler Wise (Wise AI Solutions, ABN 74 653 249 265), based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. If you have a question about your information, email tyler@wise-ai.au.

What I collect

When you complete the free AI audit or contact me, I collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business name, if you give it
  • Your answers to the audit questions, and the recommendations generated from them
  • Basic technical information your browser sends, such as browser type and the page that referred you

I do not collect payment details through this website. There are no analytics tools, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this site.

Why I collect it

  • To send you the results of your audit
  • To personally review your answers and follow up with a time for a free consult
  • To respond to enquiries you send me
  • To keep proper records of work I do for clients

I will not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for, and I will not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Where it is stored

Your submission is stored in a Supabase database and emailed to me through Resend. Both are established service providers used purely to run Wise AI, and they process your information on my behalf.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. It loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. No other third party receives your information. Some of these providers store data on servers outside Australia.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry and audit records for as long as they are useful to our working relationship, and I delete them on request.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to tell you what information I hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, or delete your information entirely. Email tyler@wise-ai.au and I will action it, usually within a few days.

Client systems

When I build automation for a client, it runs on that client's own accounts and their data stays with them. I access those systems only to build, maintain and support what I have been engaged to deliver, and I treat everything I see there as confidential.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
