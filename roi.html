The working

How each figure is worked out

Plain arithmetic, using the numbers above. A month is taken as 52 weeks divided by 12, about 4.33 weeks.

    What this assumes

    • A missed or late enquiry would have turned into work at your normal close rate. Some would never have booked anyway, which is why the close rate is in the sum.
    • Admin hours are costed at the hourly figure you enter. If the admin is yours, use what an hour of your time is worth to the business.
    • "Fixing half" is a cautious planning figure, not a promise. What's realistic depends on your business, and a call is the way to find out.
