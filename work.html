Automation case study

Giving a business its owners' time back.

Family entertainment venue · regional Queensland

From a hands-on venue to one that increasingly runs itself.

A multi-attraction family entertainment venue (bowling, arcade, café, parties and leagues), run hands-on by its two owners. They wanted the business to run more efficiently, with less repetitive manual work, so they could step back from the day-to-day. Wise AI ran a structured audit, then began automating the highest-value, lowest-risk work first.

The audit
Step 1 · Understand
Interview, observe, then prioritise

An on-site audit: one-on-one with the owners about their goals and friction, with the staff doing the actual work to find the real bottlenecks, and direct observation of how their systems are wired together and where data moves, and where it gets re-typed by hand.

Step 2 · Score
Rank every workflow on how automatable it is

Each workflow was scored on four things: how much time it eats, how repeatable it is, how structured its data is, and how risky it would be to get wrong. That turned a firehose of "everything's a problem" into a clear, ordered list of what to automate first, and what to deliberately leave alone.

What we're automating
League attendance & participation Building

The first wedge: a single, always-current view of who's in each league, who showed up each week, and payments reconciled against attendance, replacing paper, spreadsheets and memory, with nothing re-keyed. Low risk, structured data, immediate relief.

Party & group booking system In progress

A self-serve widget for birthday parties and functions with the venue's own payment checkout, so bookings and deposits come in without a staff member handling every one by hand.

Assisted roster builder In progress

An assisted draft that reads party demand and owner availability to build the weekly roster. Deliberately kept as a draft-assistant, the venue's existing rostering system stays the source of record for pay and award compliance, because that's exactly the kind of thing you don't rush.

Follow-ups, data entry & reporting Mapped & queued

Booking reminders, back-office data entry and simple performance reporting, mapped in the audit and sequenced to roll out after the first wins land.

Every automation is built on the venue's own accounts and tools, lowest-risk first, each one handing a little more of the repetitive work to the system, and a little more time back to the owners.
