What actually is an AI agent?

Think of it as a tireless team member that lives inside your software. It reads your enquiries, bookings, emails and records, makes decisions based on rules you set, and does the repetitive work for you, day and night, with no breaks, no sick days, and nothing slipping through the cracks.

What it does

What a Wise AI agent can do for your business.

One agent, quietly running the jobs that shouldn't need you. Here is the work it takes off your plate.

Answer & qualify enquiries

Replies to website, email and message enquiries instantly, any hour, answers the common questions, and captures the details of every real lead so none slip through.

Take bookings & cut no-shows

Handles bookings and enquiries around the clock, confirms them, and sends automatic reminders so fewer people forget to show up.

Follow up, every time

Chases quotes, leads and reviews on a schedule you set, in your voice, so the follow-up that usually gets forgotten after hours always happens.

Quote, invoice & chase payment

Builds an instant estimate from a few inputs, sends the quote or invoice, and follows up on what's unpaid, so cash keeps moving without you nagging.

Move data between your tools

Copies the same information across your booking, accounting and CRM tools so nothing gets re-typed five times by hand, and nothing drifts out of sync.

Report back to you

Sends a plain-English brief on what happened, what needs your call, and where the money is, so you stay across the business without living in it.
