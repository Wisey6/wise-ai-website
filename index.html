Automation case study

From paper to a business that increasingly runs itself.

A family entertainment venue in regional Queensland · bowling, arcade, café, parties, leagues

Two owners running the whole thing by hand: the weekly roster rebuilt in Excel from Sunday-night texts, every party booking a thirty-minute back-and-forth on paper, and rooms upstairs that staff could forget were occupied. The goal they set me was two days a week within three years.

  • The roster is now drafted by Claude from their own template, availability and rules — Peter reviews it and enters it into Deputy himself. Claude drafts, Peter decides, and the tool never touches pay.
  • Parties book themselves: a form with live pricing, a $100 deposit through Stripe, and the booking lands in the roster automatically. Built on Supabase and Vercel.
  • A timer app for the upstairs rooms — karaoke and the spaces out of sight of the counter — so nobody is forgotten.
